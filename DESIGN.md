# OmniBus - Design System Specification

## Color Palette
- **Obsidian Dark Surface**: `#070b08`, `#0b100c`
- **Elevated Cards & Sheets**: `#121a14`, `#162118`
- **Borders & Dividers**: `#1f2d22`, `#2b3e2f`
- **Accent Primary (Electric Volt)**: `#bef237` (contrast black `#070b08` for text on badge)
- **Text High Contrast**: `#ffffff` (Headings, primary figures)
- **Text Muted / Secondary**: `#8e9b90`, `#c4c9af`
- **Status Alerts**: `#ff7d70` (Urgent warning/Seat low), `#bef237` (Active/Ready)

## Typography
- **Headings & Key Data**: `Space Grotesk`, sans-serif (tracking-tight, bold / extrawide numbers)
- **Body & Captions**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif

## UI Architecture
1. **Header**: Clean brand identity with live route indicator, notifications, and profile access.
2. **FastLane Quick Search Bar**: Instant route switcher, departure date scroller, and quick filters.
3. **Interactive Flow**: Natural progression from Schedule Discovery -> Seat Selection -> Confirmation/Payment -> Live E-Ticket Boarding Pass.
