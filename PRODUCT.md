# OmniBus - Product Context

## Vision & Purpose
OmniBus is a modern, high-performance intercity bus transit and booking platform (Web/Mobile) designed to deliver a frictionless, executive-tier ticketing experience. It replaces generic AI-generated templates ("AI slop") with a cohesive, purposeful, production-grade interface.

## Core Features
1. **OmniBus FastLane Experience**:
   - Ultra-fast express route booking and direct express dispatch.
   - Real-time schedule monitoring and dynamic filtering (fastest, cheapest, luxury sleeper class).
   - Interactive visual cabin pod & seat selector (Multi-deck, sleeper cabins, live availability).
   - Instant digital boarding pass (e-ticket) with live gate radar, terminal directions, verified QR & baggage tag.
   - User account & VIP traveler tier management with travel histories, instant vouchers, and 24/7 terminal dispatch support.
2. **Design Language & Quality Principles**:
   - Dark mode transit aesthetic: Deep obsidian backdrop (`#070b08`, `#0b100c`), technical emerald accents (`#18231a`), and signature high-contrast electric volt lime (`#bef237`).
   - Clean, purposeful navigation without developer demo bars or redundant placeholder elements.
   - Responsive design tailored for mobile-first handheld usage and spacious responsive desktop displays.
